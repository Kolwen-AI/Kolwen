Subprocessors

These are the providers that process personal data of Kolwen's customers on Kolwen's behalf: what each one does, where it processes the data, the basis for any transfer abroad, and the data processing agreement it is under. Paddle is listed for transparency: it acts as its own controller for purchase data. Each row takes effect on the day that row's provider first holds a customer's data. Until then the row says so.

Provider

Cloudflare

Purpose

Serving the site and the service: hosting, Workers and their storage, and logs.

Personal data

Requests made to the site, as Cloudflare handles them to serve it.

Where it is processed

Cloudflare's network, and sub-processors outside the European Economic Area, Switzerland and the United Kingdom, in countries the agreement does not name.

Transfer basis

EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Module Two and Module Three); the UK Addendum; the Swiss addendum; the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework where Cloudflare is certified under it.

How long it is kept

Kept until the main agreement ends or the processing is no longer needed. After that, Cloudflare deletes or returns the data at the customer's choice, except where the law requires it to keep it. The agreement states no fixed number of days. Workers Logs, the Worker's own log lines, are kept 3 days on the Workers plan Kolwen uses at present (7 days on the paid Workers plan) until 30 November 2026, per Cloudflare's Workers pricing page, read 2026-11-08; from 1 December 2026 Workers Logs move to Cloudflare Observability pricing, and which retention applies after that date is not stated here.

Data processing agreement

Customer DPA, Version 6.4, effective 3 April 2026.

From

from the day it first holds customer data.

Provider

Supabase

Purpose

Account sign-in, and the service's database.

Personal data

Account identity (name and e-mail address) and password hashes.

Where it is processed

The project's region, chosen when the account is created, from the regions Supabase lists. The project's database and sign-in are hosted in that region. Supabase's agreement says covered data may be processed anywhere Supabase or its sub-processors maintain facilities, and its regional commitment gives way where the law or the requested service requires it. Account and support data are covered data under that rule. Service telemetry goes to systems segregated from customer projects; the agreement names no region for it.

Transfer basis

EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Module Two and Module Three), with the UK and Swiss addenda, where data leaves the EEA, the United Kingdom or Switzerland.

How long it is kept

A copy may be requested within 30 days after the agreement expires; after that the data is deleted, including copies held by sub-processors. The agreement states no number of days for completing the deletion.

Data processing agreement

Data Processing Agreement, Version 1, 1 August 2026; accepted before the account holds customer data.

From

from the day it first holds customer data.

Provider

Paddle

Purpose

Merchant of record for purchases: payment and tax. Paddle acts as its own controller for purchase data.

Personal data

The buyer's name, e-mail address, and payment and tax data.

Where it is processed

Paddle's controller entities in the United Kingdom, the United States, Ireland and Canada; its processors anywhere in the world.

Transfer basis

EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Module One, controller to controller), and the UK Approved Addendum, for transfers from the United Kingdom or the EEA to countries without an adequacy decision.

How long it is kept

No fixed period. Kept while the customer relationship continues or as the law requires, and for the limitation period and a short administrative period after it. Kept also while a claim, investigation or dispute is open.

Data processing agreement

None as a processor: Paddle acts as its own controller under its Data Sharing Addendum.

From

from the day it first holds customer data.

Provider

Resend

Purpose

Sending Kolwen's service e-mail.

Personal data

E-mail address, message content and message metadata.

Where it is processed

The United States, where Resend's primary processing takes place, and its sub-processors there.

Transfer basis

EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Module Two and Module Three); the UK SCCs; the EU SCCs as adapted for Switzerland; the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, under which Resend states it complies.

How long it is kept

Deleted within 90 days of account termination. The agreement states no period for message content or logs.

Data processing agreement

Data Processing Addendum, updated 31 December 2025.

From

from the day it first holds customer data.
